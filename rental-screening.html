How screening works

  1. Contact APLYNX and confirm the property, rental amount and availability.
  2. Review application terms and the full screening fee before agreeing to pay.
  3. Once screening is enabled, receive a secure invitation to complete identity information and consent directly with our screening provider.
  4. APLYNX reviews the completed application under consistent rental criteria and notifies you about the next step.
